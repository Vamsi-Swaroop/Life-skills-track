## Question 4
**Sleep is your Superpower**

1. Sleep is essential both before learning (to prepare the brain to absorb information) and after learning (to save and protect new memories).
2. The better we sleep, the more productive we will be.
3. Sleep loss can distort the activity of hundreds of genes, including those linked to inflammation and cancer promotion.
4. Less sleep may contribute to quicker aging.
5. We can double the amount of brain cells if we sleep consistently enough for ourselves.

---

## Question 5
**What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?**

1. Sleep in a temperature of 19 to 25 degrees Celsius.
2. Sleep in a dark room.
3. Work out regularly to get better sleep.
4. Avoid caffeine in the evenings.

## Question 6
**Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise**

1. A single workout can increase serotonin, dopamine and noradrenaline.
2. Exercise can produce new brain cells and helps in focus and long-term memory.
3. Exercise helps delay neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's and dementia, so strengthening the brain can help delay the onset of cognitive decline.
4. Exercise helps in cognitive improvement in individuals.
5. It de-stresses brain function.

---

## Question 7
**What are some steps you can take to exercise more?**

1. Start at a very basic level and do it consistently.
2. Once you can be consistent, increase the workout time.
3. Stay motivated to exercise.
4. Eat good food to feel lighter.
5. Schedule workouts at a fixed time every day (for example, a morning walk) so that exercise becomes a habit.
