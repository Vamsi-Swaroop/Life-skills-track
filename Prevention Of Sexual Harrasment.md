# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Any of the following behaviour can cause sexual harassment:

* Eve Teasing.
* Bullying
* Dirty jokes, sexual comments.
* Staring at the body and sexual gesturing.
* Pinching, patting and rubbing.
* Sending sexually offensive e-mails, messages or text messages.
* Forcible physical touch and abuse.
* Gender based insults.
* Commenting on personal attributes or physical comments.
* Sitting in an obscene manner; excessively lengthy handshakes.
* Blocking way intentionally.

## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

* Support the affected person and encourage them to file a complaint with the ICC (Internal Complaints Committee).
* Escalate to higher authorities.
* Maintain confidentiality till ICC takes necessary inquiry.
* Tell the person to stay strong mentally.
* Suggest the harassed person to admit his/her mistake and to not repeat it again.# Prevention of Sexual Harassment
