# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?

Deep Work is the practice of focusing without distraction on a single demanding task. Practising it regularly improves our cognitive ability.

---

## Question 2: According to the author, how can one do Deep Work properly?

According to the author, the following points contribute to deep work:

1. Focus fully on one important task at a time.
2. Keep your phone, social media, and other distractions away.
3. Set a fixed time for focused work and follow it regularly.
4. Take proper breaks and give your mind time to rest.

---

## Question 3: How can you implement these principles in your day-to-day life?

1. Schedule your distractions so that they do not interfere with your work schedule.
2. Aim to complete at least 4 hours of deep work per day.
3. Make your evenings a complete shutdown: free from work.

---

## Question 4: What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

Some of the dangers of social media are:

1. It wastes valuable time.
2. It distracts us from important work and goals.
3. It takes our attention away from meaningful activities.
4. People may use it because they think they need it, even when it gives little real benefit.
5. It can make us focus on appearing important rather than actually doing important things.
