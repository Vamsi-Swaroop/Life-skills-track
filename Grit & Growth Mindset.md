# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1
As per the speaker, grit means having perseverance irrespective of success or failure. It also includes constantly updating and achieving things in one's life.

## Question 2 (Introduction to Growth Mindset)
In order to have a growth mindset, one must have two things:
1. Beliefs
2. Focus

It is very important to let oneself constantly learn, put in effort, make mistakes, and learn from the mistakes for a growth mindset.

## Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
Internal Locus of Control means believing our actions and choices directly influence our results, not factors unrelated to our success.

The key point is to make good choices and believe that they are steps toward good results, instead of regretting factors that are not related to our success.

## Question 4: What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?
The major key points from the video are:
1. Believe in one's own ability to figure out things
2. Question our own assumptions
3. Create our own curriculum for growth
4. Honor the struggle
