# Learning Process

## What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a method to learn something quickly and effectively just by explaining or pretending to explain it to a child who has no prerequisite knowledge.

## Learning How to Learn (By Barbara Oakley)

The most exciting idea from the TED talk video is how important it is to use the focused mode and diffused mode together to obtain better results of learning.

Another exciting idea from this video is the emphasis on combining understanding, practice, and trying to test what we have learnt so far.

## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Active thinking** is something that we completely dedicate our thoughts to on a problem or any topic, so that we are very much determined to solve it.

**Diffused mode of thinking** refers to slow, relaxed thinking about problems subconsciously, as the brain needs to operate at its own efficiency.

## According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Deconstruct the skill
2. Learn to practice and self-correct
3. Remove any kind of practice barriers
4. Practice at least 20 hours
