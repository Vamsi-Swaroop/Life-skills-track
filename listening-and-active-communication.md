# Listening and Active Communication

## What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)

The steps for active listening are:

1. Avoiding distractions and focusing on the speaker.
2. Acknowledging the thoughts the speaker is sharing.
3. Showing appropriate body language which shows that you are listening.
4. Taking notes if required.
5. Repeating the speaker's thoughts in order to stay on the same page.
6. Letting them complete and not interrupting in the middle.

## According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)

Some key points of Fisher's model are:

* Engage in the conversation with the speaker.
* Put everything aside while the speaker is talking.
* Ask questions and let the speaker fully answer.
* Give short replies in the middle as a response.
* Keep the focus on the speaker and give the speaker the attention they deserve.

## What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Some of the distractions in the listening process are:

* Distractions in the environment or inside the head.
* Not getting the context of the topic which the speaker is talking about.

## What can you do to improve your listening?

* Indulge in the topic the speaker is talking about.
* Ponder simple questions.
* Take care of your body language so that the speaker should not feel ignored.
* Do not interrupt while the speaker is in a flow.
* Summarise the topic after the speaker completes the topic.

## When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

Some situations where we can apply passive communication are:

* When we try to differ from an opinion and acknowledge your opinion.
* When people repeatedly try to judge others.
* When we try to ask someone for help.

## When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Some cases where we can use aggressive communication are:

* When we are questioned without any mistake being done from our end.
* When someone dismisses your approval without even giving a thought about it.
* When we do not get enough support in completing the work.

## When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Some cases where we can use passive-aggressive communication are:

* When we feel ignored.
* When our opinions may get disagreed with.
* When we are treated unfairly.
* If we are in a flow of conversation and someone tries to interrupt in the middle with silly points.

## How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life? (Watch the videos first before answering this question.)

Assertive communication refers to respecting others' values but not at the cost of demeaning ourselves or our needs. Being kind and gentle, but not in return for disrespect or hate. Giving attention but not accepting recklessness from the other end. It holds space for both people's dignity at once, rather than sacrificing one for the other.
